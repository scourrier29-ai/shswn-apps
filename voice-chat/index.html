Photos in chat

The AI can ask to send a photo mid-conversation (it writes an image request into its reply). Photos are made by your image provider below — generation usually costs money per image, and most mainstream image APIs refuse adult content outright. For NSFW images you'd need a provider that explicitly allows adult content; those are typically paid and none is built in here.

Leave the key empty to disable photos. When it's set, the AI may include photos in chat — tap any photo to view it full-screen.
